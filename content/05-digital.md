# Digital Resources and Wi-Fi

## The LibraLink portal
- The LibraLink portal is the library's website for the catalog, holds, renewals, room booking and announcements.
- Sign in with your university account.
- If you forgot your university account password, the Library cannot reset it. Go to the university's main IT office (Building C, ground floor).

## E-journals and databases
- Open the "Databases" page on the LibraLink portal to see the e-journal and research databases the library subscribes to.
- On campus, access works automatically on the campus network.
- Off campus, sign in through the LibraLink portal first, then open the database from there.
- You can be signed in on up to 3 devices at the same time.
- Downloading whole journal issues in bulk is not allowed by the publishers and can get access blocked for everyone.

## E-books
- Most e-books can be read online. Some allow downloading chapters as PDF.
- Downloaded e-book loans expire after 7 days.

## Thesis repository
- Abstracts of all theses are public on the LibraLink portal.
- Full texts can be read on campus only, unless the author allowed open access.

## Library Wi-Fi
- Network name: LRU-Library.
- Sign in with your university account.
- If Wi-Fi does not work, ask the Library IT Help counter beside the print kiosks.

## Research help
- The Reference and Research Desk helps with finding sources, using databases, and citation styles (APA, MLA, IEEE).
- You can book a 30-minute research consultation with a librarian on the LibraLink portal.
