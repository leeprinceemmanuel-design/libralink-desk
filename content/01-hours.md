# Library Hours

All content in this handbook is invented for a school project. Luntian Ridge University is fictional.

## Main Library (Building A)
- Monday to Friday: 7:30 AM to 8:00 PM
- Saturday: 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM
- Sunday: closed

## Science and Engineering Branch (Building F, ground floor)
- Monday to Friday: 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM
- Saturday and Sunday: closed

## Graduate Reading Room (Main Library, 4th floor)
- Monday to Saturday: 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM
- Open to graduate students and faculty only.

## Exam weeks (midterm and final exam weeks)
- Main Library stays open until 10:00 PM, Monday to Friday.
- Main Library also opens on Sunday from 1:00 PM to 6:00 PM.
- The Science and Engineering Branch keeps its normal hours.

## Holidays and closures
- All branches close on national holidays and university-declared holidays.
- Sudden closures (for example, typhoon suspensions) are posted on the LibraLink portal announcements page.
- Online resources (e-journals, e-books, the thesis repository) are available 24 hours a day, every day.

## Last call
- The Circulation Desk stops lending books 15 minutes before closing.
- Laptops on loan must be returned 30 minutes before closing.
