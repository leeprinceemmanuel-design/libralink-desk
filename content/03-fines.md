# Fines, Lost and Damaged Items

## Overdue fines
- Regular books: 10 pesos per day, per book.
- Red Tag (reserve) books: 20 pesos per hour, per book.
- Library laptops: 50 pesos per hour late.
- The fine for one book never goes above 300 pesos (the cap). Red Tag books and laptops follow the same 300-peso cap.
- Days the library is closed (Sundays and holidays) are not counted.

## Paying a fine
1. Go to the Circulation Desk and ask for a payment slip.
2. Pay at the University Cashier using the slip.
3. Bring the official receipt back to the Circulation Desk so the fine is cleared in LibraLink.

## Lost books
- Report a lost book to the Circulation Desk as soon as possible. Overdue fines stop on the day you report it.
- You pay the current replacement cost of the book plus a 150-peso processing fee.
- If you find the book within 30 days after paying, return it and the replacement cost (but not the processing fee) is refunded.

## Damaged books
- Damage (water, torn pages, writing) is checked by Circulation staff.
- Staff decide the charge after checking the book. LibraLink Desk cannot estimate or decide damage charges.

## Clearance
- You need a cleared library account (no unpaid fines, no unreturned items) for semester clearance and graduation clearance.
- Clearance is processed by the Circulation Desk, not by LibraLink Desk.

## Appeals
- If you think a fine is wrong, fill in a Fine Appeal Form at the Circulation Desk within 14 days.
- The Circulation Supervisor reviews the appeal and replies within 5 working days.
- LibraLink Desk cannot waive, reduce, or approve fines or appeals. Only library staff can.
