# Rooms, Laptops and Printing

## Discussion rooms
- There are 6 discussion rooms on the 2nd floor of the Main Library (Rooms D1 to D6).
- Each room fits 3 to 8 people. At least 3 people must be present to use a room.
- Book a room on the LibraLink portal ("Book a Room" page), up to 7 days ahead.
- Each booking is up to 2 hours. Each group can have 1 booking per day.
- If nobody from the group arrives within 15 minutes of the start time, the booking is cancelled and the room is released.
- Rooms D5 and D6 have a TV screen with an HDMI cable for practice presentations.

## Quiet zones
- The 3rd floor of the Main Library is a silent study area. No calls, no group discussion.
- Phones must be on silent everywhere in the library.

## Laptop loans
- The Main Library has 15 laptops for use inside the library only.
- Borrow one at the Circulation Desk with your university ID.
- Loan period: up to 4 hours. Late return fine: 50 pesos per hour.
- Laptops must stay inside the library building.

## Printing, photocopying and scanning
- Print at the print kiosks on the ground floor of the Main Library.
- Black and white: 2 pesos per page. Colored: 10 pesos per page.
- Pay with your print credit, which you load at the Circulation Desk.
- Scanning to your email is free.
- Photocopying of library books must follow copyright limits: up to one chapter or 10 percent of a book.

## Food and drinks
- No food inside the library.
- Drinks are allowed only in bottles or cups with a lid, and not near the computers.

## Lost and found
- Items found in the library are kept at the Circulation Desk for 30 days.
