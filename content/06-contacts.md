## Suggesting a book to buy
- Fill in the "Suggest a Title" form on the LibraLink portal, or email Acquisitions.
- Faculty suggestions for course readings are given priority.

## Complaints and feedback
- Use the "Feedback" form on the LibraLink portal, or write to the Library Director's Office.
