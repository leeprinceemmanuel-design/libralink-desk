# Borrowing Rules

## What you need
- Your university ID works as your library card. You must show it at the Circulation Desk to borrow.
- LibraLink Desk (this assistant) cannot check your ID, your loans, or your account.

## Loan limits and periods
| Borrower type | Max books at once | Loan period | Renewals allowed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Undergraduate student | 5 | 7 days | 1 |
| Graduate student | 10 | 14 days | 2 |
| Faculty | 20 | 30 days | 3 |
| Non-teaching staff | 5 | 14 days | 1 |

## Items you cannot take home
- Red Tag (reserve) books: 3-hour loan, inside the library only.
- Reference books (dictionaries, encyclopedias, handbooks): library use only.
- Theses and dissertations (print copies): library use only, in the Graduate Reading Room.
- Newspapers and current magazine issues: library use only.

## Renewing
- Renew online on the LibraLink portal ("My Loans" page) or at the Circulation Desk.
- You can renew only before the due date.
- You cannot renew a book if another borrower has placed a hold on it.
- Each renewal gives you a new loan period of the same length as the original.

## Holds (reserving a book that is out)
- Place a hold on the LibraLink portal from the book's catalog page.
- Each borrower can have up to 3 active holds.
- When the book comes back, it is kept for you at the Circulation Desk for 3 days.
- If you do not pick it up within 3 days, it goes to the next person in line.

## Returning
- Return books at the Circulation Desk or at the book drop beside the Main Library entrance.
- The book drop is open 24 hours. Books dropped after closing are checked in the next working day, without a fine for that night.
- You may return books at either branch, no matter where you borrowed them.

## Interlibrary loan
- Graduate students and faculty can request books the library does not own through the Reference and Research Desk.
- Interlibrary loan takes about 5 to 10 working days.
- Undergraduate students cannot request interlibrary loans.
